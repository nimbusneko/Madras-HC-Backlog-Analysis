# Madras High Court Case Backlog: Analysis and Improvement Roadmap

A data-backed look at where case backlog concentrates at the Madras High Court (Principal Seat and Madurai Bench), with a KPI framework and a phased 6-month roadmap.

Author: Anshu A Meshram, BS Data Science and Applications, IIT Madras.
Prepared as a project for a Strategy & Planning (Contract) application. This is an independent academic analysis of public data, not an official Court document.

## Key findings (calendar year 2023)

- Disposals (185,343) exceeded filings (166,623): clearance rate 111.2%. Pending fell about 8.1% to 212,921 (Statistical Handbook basis).
- Writ petitions are about half of civil pending but are moving well (123% clearance, about 1.1 disposal-years).
- Risk sits in smaller, slow-moving categories: criminal appeals (52.7% clearance, stock up 22.5%), tax cases (51.8%), first appeals (87.0%) and second appeals (about 7.7 disposal-years).
- The Madurai Bench criminal side is the only unit where pendency rose (+13.0%).
- NJDG and Handbook totals differ in level (about 2.5x), so a shared data dictionary is itself a recommendation.

## Reproduce the numbers

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/compute_kpis.py
```

This reads `data/raw/hc_2023_case_flow.csv` and writes `unit_kpis.csv`, `category_kpis.csv` and `effort_scenarios.csv` to `data/processed/`. The Excel workbook holds the same calculations as formulas and includes a reconciliation sheet that checks the data against the published subtotals.

## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Clearance rate | Disposed / filed. Above 100% means the stock is shrinking. |
| Net change in pending | Closing / opening - 1 |
| Disposal-years | Closing / disposed. A proxy for years to clear the stock with no new filings at the 2023 disposal rate. It is not the age of cases. |

## Data sources

- Statistical Handbook of Tamil Nadu 2022-23, Table 31 (Department of Economics and Statistics, Government of Tamil Nadu; source data from the Registrar, IT-cum-Statistics, High Court, Chennai).
- National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) pendency figures as quoted in Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha replies.
- Press and research reports cited in the report appendix.

## Limitations

- Category-level data is for 2023. Refresh from the live NJDG and the latest Statistical Handbook before reuse.
- Disposal-years is a proxy. Scenarios assume filings stay at 2023 levels. Targets are illustrative.
- NJDG and Handbook counts use different bases; do not mix them in one calculation.
- Age-bucket data (share of cases pending more than 3 and 5 years) is not in the public documents reviewed.
